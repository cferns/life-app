# Root causes

This document lists underlying causes of daily overwhelm and misalignment, plus broader contextual determinants. It also catalogs problem areas a digital companion can realistically help with.

- **Biological energy variability**: sleep, nutrition, stress, circadian dips.
- **Cognitive bandwidth limits**: finite attention/working memory; decision fatigue.
- **Environment–brain mismatch**: hyper‑stimuli, always‑on inputs, constant novelty.
- **Fragmented systems**: tasks/calendars/notes split across tools; lost coherence.
- **Poor task design**: vague, too large, no clear next step.
- **Weak feedback loops**: little reflection or outcome tracking → slow learning.
- **Social scaffolding erosion**: less communal rhythm, accountability, and help.
- **Incentive misalignment**: apps optimize engagement, not well‑being or follow‑through.
- **Unclear priorities/values drift**: actions detach from what matters.
- **Disrupted routines/rituals**: irregular anchors reduce stability.

## Human behavioral elements

- Executive function limits: time blindness, prospective memory failures, initiation/activation, sequencing/organization, stop‑on‑time control.
- Cognitive biases and heuristics: present bias, planning fallacy, optimism bias, sunk cost, status quo bias, loss aversion, social proof.
- Motivation dynamics: expectancy × value vs delay/impulsiveness; autonomy, competence, relatedness; perceived effort/aversion.
- Emotion regulation patterns: avoidance, rumination, catastrophizing, shame loops; limited affect labeling.
- Habit mechanics: cue–routine–reward loops, friction and affordances, identity‑based habits, streak effects, extinction bursts.
- Digital compulsion loops: variable rewards, novelty seeking, cue‑triggered checking; salience hijacking.
- Social drivers: norms, reciprocity, accountability, reputation; attachment needs and belonging.
- Stress responses: fight/flight/freeze/fawn; cognitive narrowing under threat; safety behaviors.
- Transitions and temporal landmarks: start/stop friction, handoff failures, “fresh start” effects, endings.
- Relapse and repair: lapse vs relapse, abstinence‑violation, “what‑the‑hell” effect; reset rituals.
- Metacognition and self‑awareness: interoception accuracy, tracking errors, reflection cadence; calibration.
- Self‑efficacy and agency: learned helplessness, locus of control; mastery evidence and small wins.
- Identity and values coherence: value–behavior gap (akrasia), misaligned incentives; precommitment.
- Decision hygiene: option generation, criteria setting, defaulting, pre‑mortems, checklists.

## Values‑centered root causes (from project tenets)

1. **Lack of intentionality and clear prioritization:** Humans struggle to consistently choose actions aligned with their deeper values and goals amidst competing demands. This leads to decision fatigue and feeling adrift (evident in "Decision‑Making" as a primary pattern and the "Decide" core action).
2. **Disconnection from foundational human needs and purpose:** When individuals neglect "Primary Patterns" like Survival (Eat/Rest) and Social (Connect), or lack awareness of "Divine Origin" and spiritual grounding, their efforts become fragmented and less effective. The project emphasizes "love God and others better" and addressing core needs.
3. **Dependence on external tools and stimuli, rather than internal virtue:** Modern life often encourages reliance on endless tools and notifications ("tertiary patterns"), which can prevent the development of internal discipline and self‑reliance. The app's "anti‑dependence" and "fade plan" directly target this.
4. **Overload of information and tasks:** The sheer volume of daily tasks, decisions, and external inputs (the "100 daily moments of overwhelm") can exceed human processing capacity, leading to paralysis and feeling "stuck."
5. **Inadequate personal operating systems for navigating life:** Without a structured, yet flexible, approach to managing daily activities, relationships, and spiritual life, individuals default to reactive behaviors rather than proactive, virtuous living. The app aims to provide this "navigator".
