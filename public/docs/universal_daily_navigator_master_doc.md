# Universal Daily Navigator — Master Doc

One file that pulls together the full conversation: the human hierarchy, examples, app schema, UI, user archetypes, stuck moments, and faith resources.

---

## 1) Product vision (short)
A single, simple daily navigator that helps people **love God and others better**. It starts as scaffolding and **fades** as the person grows in virtue, relationships, and reliance on God/community.

- **Core actions:** Eat/Rest (Survival) • Connect (Social) • Decide (Wisdom)
- **Optional faith mode** with morning/midday/evening anchors.
- **Community-first:** surfaces help from real people before automation.
- **Built to disappear:** prompts reduce as the user gets steadier.

## 2) The hierarchy of human activity (with Divine Origin)

### 0.0 Divine Origin (Before All Layers)
**God as Creator and Sustainer** — God designed human nature and continually sustains existence. All subsequent layers flow from His providence.

### 0. Prerequisites (Before Primary)
- **Biological & Environmental Foundations** — body, brain, air, water, land, livable conditions.
- **Innate Instincts & Drives** — impulses for survival, connection, and choice-making.

### 1. Primary Patterns (Universal Daily Drivers)
- **Survival Maintenance** — eating, drinking, sleeping, staying safe.  
  _If removed:_ survival fails.
- **Social Interaction** — connecting, communicating, cooperating.  
  _If removed:_ isolation and decline.
- **Decision-Making** — choosing actions by needs/goals/values.  
  _If removed:_ no adaptation or intentionality.

### 2. Bridging Functions (Between Primary and Secondary)
- **Core Capabilities** — perception, memory, movement, communication.
- **Cultural Shaping** — traditions, norms, shared knowledge.

### 3. Secondary Patterns (Expressions of Primaries)
Work/Labor • Art/Creativity • Play/Recreation • Worship/Spiritual Practice • Learning/Teaching.  
_Why “expressions”:_ strip away the surface and you’ll find one or more primaries driving them.

### 4. Tertiary Patterns (Tools/Methods)
Note‑taking • Task management • Calendars & scheduling • Knowledge systems.  
_Why tertiary:_ optional enhancers; not universal drives; make secondaries efficient.

**Forest family example:** No notes or apps; track tasks orally, time by seasons, knowledge by stories — still meeting primary and secondary needs.

### 5. Quaternary Patterns (Extensions)
Advanced, niche systems (AI productivity, global PM, specialized medicine).

### 6. Meta‑Patterns
Self‑reflection and redesign of lower layers (life audits, continuous improvement, cultural renewal).

---

## 3) Tertiary placement for examples
- **Note‑taking** → serves Learning/Teaching + Decision‑making + collaboration.
- **Task management** → serves Work/Labor + Decision‑making.
- **Calendars** → serve Decision‑making + Work/Labor + Social coordination.

## 9) Simulation (10-user synthetic day) — insights we used
- **Completion drops** for late‑evening items and longer-effort tasks.
- **Social‑rooted tasks** tended to be deferred more than survival/decision.
- **Short actions** (≤10 min) raised completion and served as momentum starters.
- Led to **deficit‑driven Next Best Action**: score = urgency (time) + primary‑deficit + short‑boost.

---

## 10) One go‑to app blueprint (anti‑dependence)
- **MVP:** Next Best Action (deficit‑aware), three intent buttons (Eat/Rest • Connect • Decide), anchors, people list, decision log.
- **Fade engine:** Guided → Nudge → Quiet → Sabbatical; auto‑reduces prompts after steady weeks.
- **Sabbath mode:** one day/week: quiet UI, promote rest/worship/presence.
- **Metrics (aim for less dependence, more love):** fewer opens/day, more commitments honored, more acts of love per week, more decisions with outcomes learned.

---

## 11) How to use this library in‑app
- Map every task/event/note to **Primary roots** and optional **Secondary** type via `alignments`.
- When a user selects a **stuck moment**, offer 1–3 context‑aware **Next Best Actions** (micro‑steps).
- Surface **People** before tools: “who needs love today?” with one‑tap contact prompts.
- In **Faith mode**, insert anchors and short prayers; keep language gentle and optional.
